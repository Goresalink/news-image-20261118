# News Image — 18 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118):
sum up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image.

## The image

The Kerch (Crimean) Bridge on the morning of 17 November 2026, after
Ukraine's overnight missile and drone strike: the road arch and a span of
roadway have collapsed into the Kerch Strait while the railway arch still
stands. It was the second strike on the bridge in two days, on the eve of
the planned Trump–Xi–Putin meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, and the
lead story of the day's world news.

Photo: Crimean monitoring channels via UNITED24 Media / The War Zone,
as published on 18 November 2026.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13) is fully offline and deterministic: it copies the
bundled image `news.jpg` to `/output/entry.png`. Running it twice writes
identical bytes.

```
python3 main.py
# wrote /output/entry.png (83897 bytes)
```

Output: JPEG, 1024×628, 83,897 bytes (limit: 1 MiB, 1024×1024).
